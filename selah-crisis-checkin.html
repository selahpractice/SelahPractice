The Crisis Check-In

For the moment that feels bigger than you. We go slowly, one small thing at a time. Nothing here is stored or sent anywhere.

The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.Psalm 34:18

First — are you safe right now?

If you might act on thoughts of harming yourself, please call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, Canada & US) right now, or go to your nearest emergency department. In Winnipeg, the Mobile Crisis Service is 204-940-1781. Reaching out is not weakness. It is the step.

Then — ground, in this order

  • Feet: press them flat into the floor. Notice the pressure. You are in a body, in a room, in this minute.
  • Breath: two short inhales through your nose, one long exhale out your mouth. Twice.
  • Five things: name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, one you can taste.
  • Cold: hold something cold. A regulated body hears comfort better than a flooded one.

Then — one honest sentence

Say it out loud, or write it here for no one: “Right now, the truest thing is…”

Then — one person

Text one safe person one true sentence. Not the whole story. Just: “Today is a hard one. Please check on me later.” Let yourself be accompanied, even badly, even briefly.

Weeping may endure for a night, but joy comes in the morning. Tonight, enduring is enough.

After Psalm 30:5
