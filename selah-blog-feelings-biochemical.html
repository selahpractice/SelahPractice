Your Feelings Are Biochemical

6 min read - PNI - Selah Practice

When someone tells you that grief is "just in your head," they are accidentally telling the truth — and completely missing the point. Your head is command central for your immune system, your hormones, your gut, your skin, and the rhythm of your heartbeat.

Psychoneuroimmunology — PNI — is the science of the bidirectional conversation between your psychological state and your biological systems. And it is one of the most validating bodies of research for women who've been told their symptoms are "just stress."

Loneliness Is an Inflammatory Condition

This is not metaphor. Perceived social isolation increases inflammatory markers — including IL-6 and CRP — at levels comparable to physical injury. Your immune system literally responds to disconnection as a threat. Relationship is not a luxury. It is a biological necessity.

"The body keeps the score. PNI explains exactly how — and it turns out the score is written in cytokines."

Why Community Is a Health Intervention

Faith communities — when they are safe, embodied, and genuinely connective — activate the same neurobiological pathways as the best therapeutic interventions. Oxytocin from physical presence. Vagal tone from communal singing. Meaning-making that regulates the prefrontal cortex.

The science has caught up to what faith traditions have always known. Being held by something larger than yourself has a measurable effect on your biology.

The Good News

If your feelings are biochemical, that means they are real, they have measurable effects, they are not weakness — and they can change. The same pathways that allow stress to suppress your immune system allow safety, connection, and rest to restore it.
