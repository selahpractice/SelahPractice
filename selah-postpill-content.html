The Post-Pill Content Kit

Functional nutrition education, formatted for the platforms. Film the 60-second one first.

TikTok · Instagram · Ready to Film

TikTok Script 1 — 60 seconds

Hook (0–3s): “You stopped the pill and your skin, your cycle, and your mood all fell apart at once. That's not a coincidence. It has a name.”

Beats: Post-pill syndrome — the temporary cluster after hormonal birth control stops. The pill suppresses your own hormone production and depletes specific nutrients for years: B vitamins, magnesium, zinc, folate. When you stop, your body has to restart production with an empty pantry. The skin is often first to report it — androgen rebound plus zinc depletion. The cycle chaos is a system rebooting, not a body broken.

CTA: “Part two is the protocol. Follow for the science your doctor didn't have time to explain.”

TikTok Script 2 — 90 seconds (the protocol)

Hook: “The post-pill protocol nobody handed you.”

Beats: Inositol for insulin and cycle support. Zinc for skin and hormone production. Magnesium glycinate for sleep, cramps, and calm. Vitex — carefully, and not for everyone. Seed cycling as a gentle rhythm tool. Berberine for blood sugar, with respect and caution. And what's overhyped: beef organs are food, not a protocol.

CTA: “The full written protocol lives in the Selah guides. Link in bio.”

TikTok Script 3 — 30 seconds (the reframe)

Hook: “Your body is not betraying you after the pill. It is rebooting — out loud.”

Beats: Symptoms are a system coming back online, not a verdict. Give it the nutrients, the sleep, and the months it is actually asking for. 3 to 12 months is a normal recovery window.

CTA: “You are not behind. You are mid-reboot.”
