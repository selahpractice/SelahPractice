Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Who we are

Selah Practice is the integrative health practice of Pennye Eshetu, based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Canada. This website is selahpractice.ca. You can reach us any time at hello@selahpractice.ca.

What we collect

Very little, and only what you choose to give:

  • Your email address, when you voluntarily join the Selah Letter, ask to be notified about a guide or the children's book, or send an enquiry.
  • Your name and message, if you include them in an enquiry.

The Selah Check-Up runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are never transmitted to us, never stored on any server, and disappear when you close the page.

We do not run advertising trackers on this site, and we do not collect payment information here. Purchases and bookings happen on the third-party services named below, under their own policies.

How we use it

Your email is used only for what you signed up for:

  • The Selah Letter (our weekly devotional email)
  • Notifications when a guide, resource, or book you asked about becomes available
  • Session openings and replies to your enquiries

Nothing else. Your information is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Mailchimp — email list management and delivery. Mailchimp privacy
  • Gumroad — digital product sales. Gumroad privacy
  • Calendly — session booking. Calendly privacy
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. GitHub privacy
  • Payhip — Selah Studio shop. Payhip privacy

Your rights under PIPEDA

Selah Practice follows Canada's federal privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). You have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Access it, and correct it if it is wrong
  • Have it deleted, and withdraw consent at any time
  • Unsubscribe from any email with one click, in every message we send

Email hello@selahpractice.ca with any request. We respond within 30 days.

Visitors from the European Union

If you are in the EU, the GDPR gives you similar rights — access, correction, deletion, and portability of your data. We honour those requests on the same 30-day commitment. Just ask.

Health disclaimer

The Selah Check-Up is an educational self-reflection tool. It is not a medical diagnostic, and nothing on this site constitutes medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Please work with your own licensed healthcare providers for medical decisions.

Children

Our children's book, The Cracks That Shine, is a story for children, but this website is for adults. We do not collect data from or about children, and we do not knowingly accept signups from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a fresh date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the Selah Letter.
