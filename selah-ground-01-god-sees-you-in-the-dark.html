When the Ground Gives Way · Part 1 of 5

God Sees You in the Dark

When everything solid gives way, the first truth is not that you are strong. It is that you are seen.

6 min read · Faith · Presence

Hagar named God in a desert, after being used and discarded, while running from a house that had no room for her pain. She did not name Him the God who fixed it. She named Him El Roi — the God who sees me.

She gave this name to the Lord who spoke to her: "You are the God who sees me."Genesis 16:13

Before rescue, witness

When the ground gives way — the diagnosis, the loss, the job, the version of life you were building on — the first mercy is rarely rescue. It is witness. Somebody sees. Somebody stays.

God's first move with Hagar was not a new circumstance. It was His presence in the one she had. The well was already in the desert. Her eyes had to be opened to it.

For the dark you are in

You may not be able to say yet that God is good in this. Start smaller. Say that He sees in this. The dark is not evidence of His absence. Some of the most-seen people in Scripture were seen precisely there.
