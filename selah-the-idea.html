Selah · The Idea

Science is 3,000 years late.

What psychoneuroimmunology just discovered, the Bible already said.

Psychoneuroimmunology — PNI — is real science. Labs, studies, measurements. And it matters: it tells the woman living with chronic illness that what she feels in her body is not imaginary. Her nervous system, her immune system, and her inner life are in constant conversation.

But read the findings slowly, and you start to notice something. The science keeps arriving, breathless, at places Scripture has been standing for three thousand years. Breath that calms the body — Genesis. Sleep before solutions — Elijah. A crushed spirit drying up the bones — Proverbs, saying in one line what immunology needed decades to measure.

Selah exists in that meeting. Not faith against science, and not science explaining faith away — the two of them, saying true things about one whole person.

Why I can say this

The degrees gave me the map. The illness gave me the territory.

I did postgraduate studies in Clinical Psychoneuroimmunology at the Pruimboom Institute — where clinical PNI began — and I was in its first cohort. I am not a student of this science. I own it.

And I did not learn it from a textbook first. I learned it from a body that was my research: adverse childhood experiences, complex trauma, a body that learned emotional suppression as safety — psoriasis and thyroid carrying what my voice couldn't. The science gave me the language for what my body already knew.

That is the ground Selah is built on. Not a theory I admire — a map I walked.

What this means for you

You are not imagining it.

If your labs are normal and your body disagrees, you are not imagining it. If rest feels impossible and faith feels far away, you are not failing at either. Your nervous system, your immune system, and your inner life are having one conversation — and it can be learned, gently, in order.

That order is the rest of this site. It starts with a map of where the weight is sitting.
