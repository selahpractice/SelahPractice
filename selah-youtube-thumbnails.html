Thumbnail Templates

Face to camera, thinking — not performing. Keyword block on the left, three words max.

6 Templates · 10 Title/Keyword Pairs · Filming Notes

The formula

  • Face to camera, real expression — not smiling, thinking. Direct eye contact.
  • Bold keyword block on the left, Inter Black 900, high contrast. Three words max.
  • One topic per thumbnail. Dark background, one colour pop.
  • Photo fills the right 55% of a 1280×720 Canva frame.

Six templates (colour variants)

  • Amber — deep brown background, amber keyword block
  • Sage — near-black background, sage keyword block
  • Rust — deep brown background, rust keyword block
  • Near-Black — #16120d background, parchment keyword block
  • Amber Glow — warm radial glow behind the face, amber text
  • Blush Dark — deep brown background, blush keyword block

Titles → thumbnail keywords

  • Post-Pill Syndrome Explained → POST-PILL TRUTH
  • Your Gut Makes 90% of Your Serotonin → 90% SEROTONIN
  • What the Psalms Know About Your Nervous System → PSALMS + NERVES
  • Reading Your Own Labs → READ YOUR LABS
  • Wired But Tired: The Cortisol Pattern → WIRED + TIRED
  • Why Your Skin Exploded After the Pill → SKIN AFTER PILL
  • The Acanthosis Nigricans No One Explained → DARK PATCHES?
  • Chronic Fatigue Is Not Laziness → NOT LAZY
  • When Worship Doesn't Move You → NUMB IN CHURCH
  • Faith and Anxiety: 1 Peter 5:7 → CAST IT

Filming notes

  • One photo session, dark wall, six expressions — thumbnails for six videos in an afternoon.
  • Title formula: the exact question she is Googling, 10–12 words, faith + practical together.
  • Description template: two-line summary, timestamps, Check-Up link, Letter link, one Scripture line, disclaimer.
  • Channel about text: use the description from the YouTube Kit page.
