01 · Psychoneuroimmunology

The science underneath Selah

Psychoneuroimmunology — PNI — is the study of how your mind, your nervous system, and your immune system talk to each other. I teach it the way I learned to need it: plainly.

Stress is not just a feeling. It's a signal your immune system hears.

When stress runs long, the brain's alarm system talks directly to your immune system and your hormones. Cortisol, inflammation, sleep, mood — it is one conversation, not separate problems.

Your gut is in the conversation too.

The gut, the brain, and the immune system trade messages all day — through nerves, hormones, and the microbiome. It's why your digestion follows your mood, and your mood follows your digestion.

Rest is a biological event, not a reward.

A nervous system stuck on high alert cannot repair at full speed. Breath, stillness, and safety are how the body gets the signal that it can stand down — and standing down is where striving ends and being met begins. The healing itself is God's.
