about the studio

Born from living with it

Selah Studio was born from living with psoriasis and the exhaustion of always managing it. I wanted products that felt gentle — on my skin and on my senses — so I started making them.

Small-batch soaps and calming oils. Soft scents. Simple textures. Nothing loud. Just care that doesn't overwhelm.

Selah means pause, breathe, and look again. That's what this is: a quiet reminder that healing can be slow, and God can still be present in it.
