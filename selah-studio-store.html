Selah Studio

Soaps, perfumes, and oils — small, handmade doors into the same philosophy.

Handmade in Winnipeg · Shop on Payhip

Selah Studio is where the philosophy gets a scent and a weight in your hand. Small-batch soaps, perfumes, and oils, made slowly, in the same spirit as everything at Selah Practice: the body is not an afterthought, and care can be ordinary and holy at the same time.

What you'll find in the shop

  • Handmade soaps — small-batch bars, including the mini soaps made for gifts and party favours
  • Balms and oils — for skin that needs gentleness, made with the PNI eye for what touches the body
  • Perfumes — quiet, warm scents; nothing that enters the room before you do
  • Selah Cards and small goods — the tiny teachings, printed to keep

The full shop lives on Payhip, where checkout is simple and secure.
