Inside the Feeling

A five-step check-in for the moment a feeling is louder than your thoughts. Ground, name it, weigh it, trace it, let it speak — then hear what it needs. Call it emotional alchemy: a feeling, honestly met, doesn't leave the way it came.

Step 0 of 5 · Grounding
Be still, and know that I am God.Psalm 46:10

The Hebrew word is raphah — let your hands drop. Before we name anything, we arrive. Breathe with the circle: in as it grows, out as it settles. Three rounds is enough.
